About KindBytes

One platform for surplus food and food waste

Hotels, messes and hospitals list surplus in under a minute. KindBytes grades it for safety, sends edible food to people, and routes the rest to animal feed, biogas or recycling, so nothing reaches landfill.

How it works

From kitchen to people in under an hour

  1. 01

    List and grade

    A kitchen lists surplus. The safety engine checks freshness, temperature, packaging and hygiene, and grades it A, B or C.

  2. 02

    Segregate at source

    Each batch splits into four streams: food for people, animal feed, wet waste and dry packaging.

  3. 03

    Match and move

    The nearest NGO with room gets the food. A hygiene-checked volunteer carries it, and every handover is scanned.

  4. 04

    Prove it

    Bulk kitchens get a monthly off-site processing certificate for their municipal body.
